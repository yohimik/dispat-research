## The defect
Today a unit u at commit C carrying feat(P)^ is admitted for a reached consumer d iff `C ∈ Wfresh(d)` (d has not released past C). Chain: b consumes a; commit C carries feat(a)^ and feat(b). Run 1: a fails, b proceeds on its own bump (the own-reason exception in `release/executor.go` `shouldSkip`) and tags at HEAD past C. Run 2: a publishes C. Run 3: b is never planned again, because b's tag is past C. b stays on a's old version for ever and no diagnostic fires: an orphan. The same happens to a consumer that overtakes a held provider with a release of its own.

## The rule (from the specification owner, verbatim in substance)
1. Bump axis only; the channel axis keeps `C ∈ Wfresh(d)`.

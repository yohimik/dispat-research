## The defect (confirmed by gate W's agent by draining the task graph by hand)
`services/dispat/internal/release/executor.go` builds the task graph from `p.Providers[name]` filtered by `changed[prov]`: a consumer's publish waits only for the publishes of its CHANGED DIRECT providers, and `shouldSkip` (same file) looks only at direct providers' results. With `app -> ui -> core`, `app` and `core` in the plan and `ui` carrying nothing to release (the common incremental case):
1. nothing orders `publish(core)` before `publish(app)`: once `build(app)` is done both publishes are ready and mutually unordered, so `app` can publish first, against a `core` that has not published (SPEC §19.2: "Order over the whole graph, then filter, never over the induced subgraph"; vector 80a);
2. if `core`'s publish fails, `app` is not blocked (W194), because `core` is not among its direct providers (SPEC §19.3: "The closure is computed over the full workspace graph, so it traverses packages that are not in the plan").
Both halves are one change and must land together: ordering without blocking would publish `app` after a `core` that failed.

\begin{abstract}
Releasing interdependent packages is a distributed transaction without a
coordinator or rollback: each publish is an irreversible write to an
independent registry. CCME defines a resumable release algorithm and states
eight guarantees: termination, completeness, version stability, exactly-once
tag-ledger delivery, retry-invariant non-widening and convergence, channel
discharge and one-way axis independence.

This report formalises the 2.0.0 algorithm and proves the guarantees under its
explicit retry invariant, together with the determinism and idempotency requirements of
\spec{17.2}, the duality of \spec{13.7b}, and analyses the cost and optimisation bounds of \specs{13.11}{20.7}, qualifying them where ancestry and output work add terms. It preserves an audit of six failure classes in the
1.0.0 rules, with minimal counterexamples, repairs, and conformance vectors.
CCME 2.0.0 incorporates those six repairs; the historical audit is provenance. A separate current obligation records an inconsistency between the specific graduation rule of \spec{11.5} and its \spec{13.9} summary.
\end{abstract}

# Research work rules

- English `paper/saner/` is canonical for the active conference paper. Russian translation and synchronization are out of scope at the user’s request. The full English technical report is a separate document.
- Keep the conference paper at ten content pages plus two references; verify the PDF and visually inspect changed pages.
- Preserve raw experimental observations and failed attempts. Corrections need a new run with explicit provenance, not edited outcomes. Distinguish assertions, repetitions, generated cases, and independent samples.
- Follow each campaign's pinned binary and environment. Do not mix measurements from different versions as one run.
- Do not submit to conferences, publish artifacts, change privacy, or release Dispat without explicit authorization.
- Patent and lawyer material belongs outside this repository.
- Preserve historical absolute paths in evidence; make new executable tooling relocatable.

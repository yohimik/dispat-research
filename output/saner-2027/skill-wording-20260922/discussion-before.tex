\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}
\paragraph{Meaning of the guarantees.}
Safety differs from eventual progress~\cite{safety}. Our retry theorem fixes
corrected intent, source membership, admission predicates, and channel inputs.
Some stability lemmas follow directly from this invariant, exposing what retries
must preserve. New commits, policy edits, or tag rewrites can require replanning;
permanent failure need not converge.

The current group profile adds member target floors and independent
counter/channel axes. Fixed shared counters can advance on retry and reselect
successful members. Its independent-counter guarantees are a specification
contract; neither group mode is proved by our per-package theorem.

The model abstracts artifact contents and combines publication and recording
into one transition. Lost responses and failed recording remain integration
obligations; Git locks do not make registries transactional. Own-reason failure
exceptions, recording, and finalization require refinement beyond the abstract
blocked-set rule; selected test traces do not close that gap.

\paragraph{Internal and construct validity.}
The author developed the system, model, oracle and tests; agreement can retain
shared errors. The restricted oracle omits semantics; full-profile categories
are heuristic. The prospective matrix was frozen before execution but was not
independently replicated. The finite projection covers stable, one-intent states;
post-write faults are responses, not crashes. Integrity and concurrency checks
cover selected records and interleavings. Histories, rows, episodes, repetitions,
commands and assertions remain distinct units.

\paragraph{External validity.}
Synthetic fixtures establish controlled behavior; historical replay reconstructs
policies, and comparisons pin configurations. No user study measures intent
correctness or review effort. The control-history polyrepo fits the model;
multi-history coordination, worker transport, peer choreography, and their scale remain
unevaluated. Platform scripts, agent gates, and ecosystems demonstrate
integration scope, not general effectiveness. One contended host cannot
establish service-level promises.

\paragraph{Exploratory outreach context.}
Eight September inventories cover at least 211 deduplicated repositories with
unequal review depth; one spans 21 ecosystems. A separate search's 6,731 hits
across 1,512 repositories are discovery coverage, not defect counts.
RelayCast (9 stars) and Paperclip (80,381) illustrate visibility, not code size.
A two-clone RelayCast fixture simulates upload followed by rejected push:
no remote tag appears, yet the script reports success. No production failure
is claimed. Two observed Astro histories publish npm packages, fail before
marketplace publication, then finish green on retry while skipping those
destinations; later manual completion was not assessed. An Apache Arrow
fixture with a stubbed workflow client selects a stale successful run.
These cases motivate destination completion and invocation identity.
Healthy, resolved, and later excluded cases remain recorded. This selected
review neither estimates defect prevalence nor validates the protocol.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Release and build systems.}
Release engineering~\cite{adams}, monorepo practice~\cite{potvin}, and build-system
models~\cite{mokhov,pluto} supply the setting; we claim no new topological or
incremental-build algorithm. Lerna, Nx, Changesets, release-please and
semantic-release~\cite{lerna,nx,changesets,releaseplease,semanticrelease} offer
versioning/publication workflows; Section~\ref{sec:compare} compares pinned
configurations, not complete tools. Spinnaker's execution histories~\cite{spinnaker},
Nix's input-derived paths~\cite{nix}, TOSCA's lifecycle interfaces~\cite{tosca}
and OpenGitOps reconciliation~\cite{gitops} retain their own state models.
Our ledger tracks completed releases across independent publication targets.

Versioning studies motivate explicit policy~\cite{raemaekers,bogart,decan},
without proving compatibility. EDOS studies installability~\cite{edos};
CI effects depend on context~\cite{huangci}.
DORA associates greater AI adoption with higher delivery throughput and
instability~\cite{dora2025}. A 2026 qualitative analysis of 1,110 Google
engineers' responses collected in 2025 describes verification and production
integration costs that can offset AI generation gains~\cite{dora2026tensions}.
Our accompanying agent guide directs agents to
inspect deterministic plans, retain tests, invoke CI releases, and reconcile
partial outcomes. The engine implements a model with eight conditional
mathematical guarantees; agent effectiveness and full implementation refinement
remain unproven. DORA motivates this design, not its validation.
Workflow duplication motivates shared configuration~\cite{cardoen};
maintenance gains remain unmeasured. Formal VCS models~\cite{swierstraloh,hpt,mimram,mrdt}
and Git-mining cautions~\cite{birdgit} address concerns beyond our release ledger.

\paragraph{Recovery and formal assurance.}
Buildbox formalizes build, install, ship, and update transitions~\cite{liusmith};
Aeolus studies component states, dependencies, and reachability~\cite{aeolus}.
NEPHELE applies sagas to distributed lifecycle operations~\cite{nephele};
Bui et al. study LLM-assisted CI/CD repair~\cite{bui}.
Sagas~\cite{sagas}, transaction limits~\cite{gray}, and independently committed,
idempotently processable operations~\cite{helland07,helland12} motivate recovery
and explicit treatment of uncertain outcomes. We specialize admission and
discharge to versioned consumers. Anvil~\cite{anvil} verifies Kubernetes
controller implementations; our handwritten model proofs and sampled execution
tests provide weaker assurance. Fuzz-evaluation cautions~\cite{klees} likewise
limit generalization from core agreement.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Stage prerequisites enforce availability; consumer windows retain obligations
after provider success. The retry invariant preserves versions and permits
convergence. All 140 mechanism episodes retained expected plans; the
source-window counterfactual lost work in 84/120 fault episodes. Repository-qualified
boundaries and verified worker inputs extend the design; multi-history recovery,
recording failures and randomized interleavings need evaluation and mechanized
refinement~\cite{lamport}.
